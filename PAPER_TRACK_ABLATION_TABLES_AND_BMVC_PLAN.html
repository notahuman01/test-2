TAG Original Hyperparameter Proof

This is the anti-"just tuned w/k/r" proof.

TAG Figure 4 reports the original strong hyperparameter point as:

temporal_window_size: 21
kmeans_k: 9
window_radius: 7

Our tuned Charades geometry later uses w=13,k=12,r=8, so this proof is critical. The question is:

Do the algorithmic modules still improve when forced to use TAG's original w=21,k=9,r=7?

The answer is yes.

Recommended Paper Position

Use this language:

We do not replace TAG's candidate-generation philosophy. Instead, we show that TAG-style zero-shot VTG leaves a large final-selection gap: high-quality candidates often exist in the top-k pool but are not selected as top-1. We introduce lightweight numeric confirmation modules that improve final candidate selection without training and without LLMs. Query-similarity pooling is presented as a Charades-specific semantic smoothing extension, while LLM proposal pooling is reported separately as an assisted setting.

Avoid this language:

We tune TAG.
We use LLMs for the main method.
Query-similarity is universal.
Every module independently improves monotonically.

The strongest BMVC-safe story is the candidate-confirmation bottleneck story, backed by TAG-hyperparameter controls, top-k oracle gap, OOD validation, and clean separation between no-LLM and LLM-assisted results.
