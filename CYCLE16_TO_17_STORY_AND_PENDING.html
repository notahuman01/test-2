Cycle16 to Cycle17 Memo

Stabilization, Final Candidate, and Validation Gate

Cycle16 established the aligned TAG-derived architecture. Cycle17 refined the onset mechanism, opened LLM and query-similarity pooling candidates, and now has one last decision gate before broad experimentation should stop.

Charades clean candidate

query_similarity_pool reached 48.77 mIoU, improving the onset_abs anchor by +0.32.

ActivityNet transfer issue

The same tau=0.10 setting dropped ActivityNet from 37.91 to 37.60 mIoU.

Final gate

After the tau sweeps, stop broad experimentation and move into validation only.

Short Story

Cycle16 stabilized the shared architecture and showed the main Charades gains come from onset + refinement.
Cycle16 proved the architecture also improves under TAG's original hyperparameters.
Cycle17 moved the best Charades onset from positive gradient to absolute gradient.
Cycle17 then found two candidates: LLM-expanded query usage and query-similarity temporal pooling.
The final open question is whether query-similarity pooling has a tau that transfers to ActivityNet.
